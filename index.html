Privacy Policy

App: WhatsScheduler Pro

Effective Date: August 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how WhatsScheduler Pro ("the App", "we", "our", or "us") handles information when you use the application.

1. Information We Collect

WhatsScheduler Pro is designed to operate primarily on your own device. We do not operate our own servers and do not require you to create an account.

The app may access information that you choose to provide, including:

  • WhatsApp contacts or groups that you select.
  • Scheduled messages you create.
  • Message templates and auto-reply rules.
  • Images or media that you attach to scheduled messages.
  • Voice recordings converted into text (processed on your device).

This information remains on your device unless you explicitly share it.

2. Permissions Used

The application may request permissions such as:

  • Contacts
  • Notifications
  • Storage / Photos / Media
  • Accessibility Service (required for automation features)
  • Microphone (for voice-to-text)

These permissions are used solely to provide the requested functionality.

3. How Information Is Used

The information is used only to:

  • Schedule WhatsApp messages.
  • Send scheduled messages.
  • Create automatic replies.
  • Manage reminders.
  • Convert speech into text.
  • Display your scheduled content.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

We do not share your personal information with third parties except when required by law or when necessary to provide services that you explicitly request.

5. Advertising

The current version of WhatsScheduler Pro does not display advertisements. Future versions may include Google AdMob or other advertising services. If advertising is added, this Privacy Policy will be updated before those features are released.

6. Data Storage

Your scheduled messages, settings and related information are stored locally on your device.

We do not maintain our own cloud servers for storing your personal data.

7. Security

We use reasonable security measures to protect information stored by the app. However, no method of electronic storage is completely secure.

8. Children's Privacy

The application is not directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page together with a revised Effective Date.
